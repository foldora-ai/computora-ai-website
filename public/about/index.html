About Computora AI

Computora AI is the software publisher behind Foldora AI, Cleanora AI, and Galoria AI. These desktop applications address mixed-file organization, metadata-based folder cleanup, and local photo organization respectively.

Our approach

We build focused tools instead of adding another layer of complexity. Each product starts with a practical problem, keeps the workflow clear, and uses AI where it is genuinely useful.

A growing family of tools

Foldora AI, Cleanora AI, and Galoria AI are the beginning of a broader family of intelligent desktop applications.

Product information and support

Foldora is available for Windows 10/11 and macOS on Intel or Apple Silicon. Cleanora and Galoria are Windows applications. Compare the product workflows, supported platforms, and current prices before choosing an app or the complete bundle.

Each product has a dedicated website containing its current platform support, workflow, file-format guidance, privacy explanation, and purchase link. Computora AI publishes the complete product-family overview and provides customer support across the three applications.

For support, contact support@computoraai.com.
